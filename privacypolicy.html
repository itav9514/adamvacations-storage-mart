Privacy Policy for My Storage Cart

Personal Information:

Name, email address, phone number, and billing information when you create an account or make a purchase.

Non-Personal Information:

IP address, browser type, operating system, and browsing behaviour, which are collected automatically through cookies and other tracking technologies.

Payment Information:

Payment card details and related financial information are processed securely through our payment gateway providers and are not stored on our servers.
